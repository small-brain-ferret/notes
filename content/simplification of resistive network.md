> [!NOTE] rationale of the method
> We can convert a network of resistors with many nodes and branches into a simpler network using the concept of **equivalent resistance**.
>
> ![[Attachments/Pasted image 20260911224118.png]]
>
> Two parallel resistors:
>
> $$
> R_{eq}=\frac{R_{1}R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}
> $$

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Resistors are parallel only if both ends are connected to the **same two nodes**.
