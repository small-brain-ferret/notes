> [!NOTE] annealing
> Recover the [[ductility]] of a metal, reversing [[work hardening]].
>
> This reversal doesn't change the shape.
>
> There are three stages upon heating up the sample:
>
> 1. Recovery - diffusion of atoms, which repairs some of the [[dislocations]] to annihilate each other to form a perfect plane. [[dislocations]] density drops.
> 2. Recrystallisation - forming more perfect crystals, dislocation density will drop further.
> 3. Grain growth - there will still be [[dislocations]]
