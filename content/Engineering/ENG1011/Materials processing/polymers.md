> [!NOTE] polymers
> Long chains, typically long carbon backbones.
>
> A repeat structure made of many units.
>
> - covalent bonds within the chain
> - secondary bonding: [[van der Waals bonding]] & [[hydrogen bonding]] between chains

# Polymer types

- Linear ([[thermoplastic]])
- branched (thermoplastic)
- crosslinked ([[thermoset]] or elastomer)
- network (thermoset)

# Applications

- aerospace - low density, very lightweight, excellent thermo properties (good insulator)
- biomed - contact lenses (hydrogel)
- electronics - photoresists, new materials allows smaller features to be packed, increase number of transistors
- bank notes - Australian invention, harder to counterfeit
