We can add a layer at a time to make a component.
This opposes reductive manufacturing, where we take parts away from a big blocks.

- rapid prototyping
- individually-customised products (one-of-a-kind)
- design features that can't be made with other techniques
- reducing waste compared to subtractive manufacturing
