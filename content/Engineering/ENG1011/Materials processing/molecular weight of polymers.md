For linear [[polymers]], low MW means a shorter chain, high MW means a longer chain.

- Very low MW: oils/waves
- high MW (more chain entanglements): plastics eg. tensile strength and impact strength generally increases with MW

polymers tend to have a distribution of MWs. Statistics are used to determine material properties.
