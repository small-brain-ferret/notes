> [!NOTE]
> A distortion in a crystal lattice along a line, typically found in metals, affecting [[yield stress]].

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-19 at 14.59.58.png]]

The dislocation allows [[slip]] to proceed at [[stress]] lower than the predicted [[yield stress]].

Disruptions to the system caused by moving a dislocation is far less than that would be required to move the whole atom planes. (i.e. sliding one entire plane of atoms over another all at once would require a very large force, because many [[atomic bonding|atomic bonds]] would need to be disturbed simultaneously.)

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-19 at 15.06.40.png]]
As a dislocation moves in the vicinity of another dislocation, they will repel one another. The energy barrier is too large and it has become repulsive.
