[[dislocations]] are the mechanism for [[slip]] planes to move across one another.

No dislocations = perfect crystal:

- high [[yield stress]]
- low [[ductility]]

Few dislocations = dislocations can move freely

- low [[yield stress]]
- high [[ductility]]

High numbers of dislocations = dislocations can not move:

- high [[yield stress]]
- low [[ductility]]
