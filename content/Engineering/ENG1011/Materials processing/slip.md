Important for understanding [[plastic deformation at an atomic level]].

If we apply a force to try to move two planes over one another, we must rupture the bonds between atoms.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-18 at 22.30.46.png]]

The [[yield stress]] required to do this can be calculated.

It is about [[Young's Modulus E|E]]/15 - extremely high.
