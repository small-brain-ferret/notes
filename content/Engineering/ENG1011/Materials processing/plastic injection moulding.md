A contrasting manufacturing method to [[3D printing]], injection moulding is most useful for high-volume production, as the tools must be individually configured for each part.

> [!NOTE]
> The most common method for mass manufacturing plastic products.
>
> e.g. chairs, toys, cases, disposable cutlery
>
> 1. melt plastic
> 2. inject it into a mould
> 3. let it cool

All injection moulded objects start with plastic pellets. They can be mixed with colorant or 15% recycled material.
