> [!NOTE]
> $e^-$ continuously move within atoms and molecules.
>
> At a particular instant, the $e^-$ may be distributed unevenly, creating:
>
> - a slightly negative region
> - a slightly positive region
>
> This temporary uneven charge distribution is a fluctuating dipole.
>
> The distribution may then change or reverse.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-18 at 21.33.36.png]]

This temporary dipole can induce a [[dipole]] in a neighbouring atom or molecule.

The attraction between these dipoles produces a weak [[van der Waals bonding|van der Waals bond]].
