> [!NOTE]
> Relatively weak attractions produced by uneven distributions of electrical charge.
>
> They are much weaker than:
>
> - [[ionic bonding]]
> - [[covalent bonding]]
> - [[metallic bonding]]
>
> The main secondary bonds are:
>
> - [[van der Waals bonding]]
> - [[hydrogen bonding]]
>
> Secondary bonding is particularly important in polymers.

> [!important]\
> Secondary bonds are weak individually, but a large number of them can strongly influence a material’s stiffness, melting temperature, boiling temperature, and deformation behaviour.
