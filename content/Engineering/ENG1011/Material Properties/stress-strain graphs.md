A [[Simplified bi-linear stress-strain curve]] is used when designing using ductile materials.
Used for evaluating [[Mechanical property]]. See [[stress]] and [[strain]].

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-05-23 at 18.19.38.png|328]]

> [!NOTE] Regions of a stress-train graph
>
> - [[elastic region]] - recoverable deformation
> - [[plastic region]] - non-recoverable deformation

- Independent of sample dimensions unlike [[load displacement curve]].
- For any material, regardless of dimension, the stress-strain graph should look the same
- This makes it useful for comparing different materials.
