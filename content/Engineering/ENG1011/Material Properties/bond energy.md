> [!NOTE]
> Bond energy describes the interaction between two atoms as a function of the distance between them.
>
> - negative energy is attractive
> - positive energy is repulsive
> - zero energy is no interaction

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-18 at 21.39.42.png]]

## Atoms far apart

When atoms are infinitely far apart, there is no interaction between them.

Therefore:

$$
U=0  
$$

## Atoms moving closer

As atoms move towards each other, they begin to attract.

The bond energy decreases until it reaches a minimum.

## Atoms too close together

When atoms are forced too close together, the interaction becomes repulsive.

The bond energy then increases very rapidly and becomes positive.

## Equilibrium distance

The [[equilibrium interatomic distance]] between neighbouring atoms is represented by:

$$
r_0  
$$

At this distance, the bond energy is at its minimum.
