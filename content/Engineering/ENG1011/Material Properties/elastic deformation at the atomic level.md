When a load is applied to a material within its [[elastic region]], the material changes length.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-18 at 21.37.24.png]]

At the atomic scale, this can be imagined as a small change in the distance between neighbouring atoms.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-18 at 21.37.38.png]]
The applied load stretches the [[atomic bonding|atomic bonds]] slightly and increases the average distance between atoms.

The [[atomic bonding|atomic bond]] between atoms can be represented approximately as a spring.

$$
\frac{F}{A_{o}}=E \frac{\Delta L}{L_{o}}
$$

LHS: [[stress]] imposed
