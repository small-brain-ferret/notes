> [!NOTE] definition
> a type of secondary bonding caused by attraction between [[temporary dipole]] or [[permanent dipoles]].

> [!NOTE] properties
> Materials dominated by van der Waals forces often have:
>
> - low [[melting point]]
> - low boiling point
> - low stiffness ([[Young's Modulus E]])
> - low [[electrical conductivity]]
> - low [[thermal conductivity]]

> [!NOTE] In polymers
> Van der Waals forces commonly act between neighbouring [[polymer chains]].
>
> ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-18 at 21.34.28.png]]
>
> Although each attraction is weak, many attractions can act along the length of the chains.
>
> This gives the polymer cohesion while still allowing some chain movement.
>
> Weak van der Waals bonding between polymer chains contributes to the relatively low stiffness and melting temperature of many polymers.
