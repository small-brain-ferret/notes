The bond-energy curve changes according to the strength of the [[atomic bonding]].

## Weak bonding

A relatively weak bond produces a shallower bond-energy curve.

The atoms are easier to pull apart slightly.

This produces:

- more deformation
- a smaller [[Young's Modulus E]]

## Strong bonding

A stronger bond produces a deeper bond-energy curve.

The atoms are harder to pull apart.

This produces:

- less deformation
- a larger [[Young's Modulus E]]

> [!important]\
> A material with a large bond energy is harder to deform and therefore generally has a higher elastic modulus.
