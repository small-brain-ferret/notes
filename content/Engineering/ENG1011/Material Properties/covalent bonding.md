A type of [[atomic bonding]].

> [!NOTE]
> Occurs when neighbouring atoms share $e^-$.
>
> A single covalent bond normally consists of two shared $e^-$.

An extended network of covalent bonds constructs [[network covalent material]].

> [!NOTE] Properties
> covalent bonds generally have high [[bond energy]].
>
> Materials dominated by covalent bonding commonly have:
>
> - high [[Young's Modulus E]] (stiffer)
> - high strength
> - high [[melting point]]
> - low [[electrical conductivity]]
>
> commonly found in:
>
> - ceramics
> - semiconductors
> - diamonds
> - organic molecules
> - individual [[polymer chains]]

> [!NOTE] Why is covalent bonding directional?
> Shared electrons occupy particular positions between specific atoms.
>
> Covalent bonds, therefore have preferred orientations in 3D space.
