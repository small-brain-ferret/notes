> [!NOTE] Observations
> In the elastic region, it will elongate uniformly.
>
> As we go past [[yield stress]], it will continue to uniformly elongate (ie. changing dimensions equally across the sample).
>
> In this uniform elongation area, as we [[plastic deformation at an atomic level|plastically deform]] it, the [[dislocations]] density increases - we are [[work hardening]] it. The material can undergo higher loads up to the [[Ultimate Tensile Strength|UTS]].
>
> As we go past the [[Ultimate Tensile Strength|UTS]], the [[stress]] overtakes the work hardening, and all deformation is localized within a particular region, causing it to form a neck.

This process is called [[necking]] - occuring all the way up to fracture, which occurs in the neck region.
