> [!NOTE]
> A polymer contains long molecular chains.
>
> There are two relevant levels of bonding:
>
> 1. Bonding within a chain
>    Atoms within an individual polymer chain are joined by strong [[covalent bonding]].
>
> 2. Bonding between chains
>    Neighbouring chains are held together by weaker [[secondary bonding]].
>
>    Although each secondary bond is weak, many such bonds can collectively keep the polymer chains together.
