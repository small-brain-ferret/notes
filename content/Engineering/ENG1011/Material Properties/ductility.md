> [!NOTE] ductility
> The amount of plastic [[strain]] that occurs in the sample, after [[fracture point]], is known as ductility.

> [!NOTE] Formula
> Can be expressed quantitatively as either percentage elongation or percentage reduction in area.
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \text{\%EL} & =\left( \frac{l_{f}-l_{0}}{l_{0}} \right)\times 100 \\
> \text{\%RA} & =\frac{A_{0}-A_{f}}{A_{0}}\times 100
> \end{align}
> $$
