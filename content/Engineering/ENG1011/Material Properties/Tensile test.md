Used to determine the mechanical properties of the materials to help materials selection of a component.

> [!NOTE]
> A tensile testing machine is designed to elongate the specimen at a constant rate, and to continuously and simultaneously measure the instantaneous applied load (with a load cell) and the resulting elongation (using an extensometer)

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-05-23 at 17.52.31.png|207]]

It pulls the specimen, and measures how much force is being applied with a load cell, and how much the specimen has been stretched.

This produces a plot which looks like the following - a [[load displacement curve]].

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-05-23 at 17.52.46.png|423]]

To compare specimens with the same materials but different dimensions (ie. a different thickness of a specimen would result in different loads required for deformation), we must use the derived quantities of [[stress]] and [[strain]].![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-05-23 at 17.59.16.png|396]]
