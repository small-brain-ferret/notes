> [!NOTE] Definition
> Ratio of the load ($F_{\text{output}}$) to the input force ($F_{\text{input}}$).
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \text{MA} & =\frac{F_{\text{load}}}{F_{\text{input}}}=\frac{d_{\text{input}}}{d_{\text{output}}} \\ \\
>
> W_{\text{input}} & =W_{\text{output}} \\ \\
>
> F_{\text{input}}d_{\text{input}} & =F_{\text{output}}d_{\text{output}}
> \end{align}
> $$
>
> There is a tradeoff between force and the distance it is applied over since work is conserved.
>
> When we say we use [[Machines]] to make work "easier", it does not mean less work.
>
> - energy/work ($W$) is conserved in ideal [[Machines]]
>
>   MA > 1 means output force will be larger than input force, but input force needs to be applied over a greater distance.
