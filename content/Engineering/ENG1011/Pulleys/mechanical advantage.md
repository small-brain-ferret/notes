> [!NOTE] Definition
> Ratio of the load ($F_{\text{output}}$) to the input force ($F_{\text{input}}$).
>
> $$
> \text{MA}=\frac{F_{\text{load}}}{F_{\text{input}}}=\frac{d_{\text{input}}}{d_{\text{output}}}
> $$
>
> There is a tradeoff between force and the distance it is applied over since work is conserved.
