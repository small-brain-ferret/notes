Linear [[springs]] are governed by Hooke's law, which states that deformation ($x$) of a spring over the [[elastic region]] (and plastic region only) is directly proportional to the applied force ($F$) via a spring constant/stiffness ($k$).

Deformation in the [[plastic region]] doesn't follow Hooke's law.

> [!NOTE] Hooke's law
>
> $$
> F=\text{k}x
> $$
>
> i.e. applying a force of F results in an extension of x. Applying a force two times that will result in an extension two times x.

The value of the spring constant is dependent on the type of material used and its shape, which can be measured experimentally.
