# Example question

The parallel spring system is used to suspend block A, which has a mass of 10 kg. The springs have stiffnesses $K_{1}=70$ N/m, $K_{2}=30$ N/m, $K_{3}=50$ N/m. Determine the extension experienced by the springs.

##### Step 1: Determine the equivalent spring constant of the parallel spring system.

$$
\begin{align}
K_{e} & =K_{1}+K_{2}+K_{3} \\
 & =70+30+50 \\
  &=150\ \text{N/m}
\end{align}
$$

##### Step 2: Determine the total elastic force experienced by  the system using [[Hooke's law]].

$$
\begin{align}
F_K & =K_e \ x \\
 & =150x\ \text{N}
\end{align}
$$

##### Step 3: Apply the [[equilibrium]] equation in the vertical direction.

$$
\begin{align}
\sum F_{y} & =0 \\
F_{k}-mg & =0 \\
150x-10(9.8) & =0 \\
x & =0.6533\ \text{m}
\end{align}
$$
