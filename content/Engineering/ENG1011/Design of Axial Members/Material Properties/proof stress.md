$\sigma_{0.2}$

> [!NOTE] Finding proof stress using [[stress-strain graphs]]
> Step 1: Move out from the origin a nominal 0.2% or 0.002 strain
> Step 2: Draw a line parallel to the initial slope
>
> Where the line intersects the curve is known as the 0.2% proof stress.
>
> 0.1% can also be used, doesn't matter too much, just remember to indicate.
