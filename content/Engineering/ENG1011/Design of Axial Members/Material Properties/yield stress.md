An important [[material property]].

> [!NOTE] Definition
> $\sigma_{y}$ is the stress at which permanent deformation occurs.
>
> At this point, there is permanent deformation in the specimen.

See [[permanent deformation]].

> [!NOTE] Note
> The yield stress is often difficult to determine directly from a stress strain plot as it is difficult to locate the transition of regions.
>
> Thus, we use the [[proof stress]].
