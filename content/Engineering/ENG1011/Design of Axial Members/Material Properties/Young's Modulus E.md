An important [[material property]].

> [!NOTE] Definition
> A material property that measures the stiffness or elasticity of a material
>
> $$
> \sigma=\text{E}\times{\epsilon}
> $$
>
> The [[stress]] is directly proportional to the [[strain]] multiplied by this modulus.
>
> $$
> E=\frac{\text{Stress}}{Strain}=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon}
> $$
>
> Determines the deformation that will occur within the elastic region.
> High modulus = less [[strain]]/elongation for a given stress
