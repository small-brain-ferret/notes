A [[Mechanical property]].
See [[stress-strain graphs]].

> [!NOTE] Definition
> Linear deformation per unit length.
> BUT
> Strain is a dimensionless quantity! (It has no units)
> Can sometimes be represented as a percentage.
>
> $$
> \epsilon=\frac{\text{Change in length, }\Delta L}{\text{Original length, }L}
> $$

# Engineering (Nominal) Strain

The change in length over the original length.

> [!NOTE] Formula
> ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-05-23 at 18.10.01.png]]
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \epsilon_{n}  & = \frac{\Delta l}{l_{0}} \\
>  & =\frac{l_{f}-l_{0}}{l_{0}}
> \end{align}
> $$
>
> where:
> $l_0$ is the original length
> $l_f$ is the final length
> $\Delta l$ is the change in length
