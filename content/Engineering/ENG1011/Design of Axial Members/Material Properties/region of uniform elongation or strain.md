> [!NOTE] Observations
> In the elastic region, it will elongate uniformly.
>
> As we go past [[yield stress]], it will continue to uniformly elongate (ie. changing dimensions equally across the sample).
>
> As we go past the [[Ultimate Limit State]], all deformation is localized within a particular region, causing it to form a neck. This process is called [[necking]] - occuring all the way up to fracture, which occurs in the neck region.
