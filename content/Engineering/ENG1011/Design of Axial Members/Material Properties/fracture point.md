> [!NOTE] When does fracture occur?
> If we maintain a [[stress]] past the [[Ultimate Tensile Strength]] of the material, fracture will occur within the neck region.

It is of little practical importance in structural engineering, as we are more interested in the [[yield stress]] and [[Ultimate Tensile Strength]].

> [!NOTE] OOOOO
> Just as the specimen fractures, the sample snaps back a bit and becomes smaller.
>
> The elastic [[strain]] at the start of the [[Tensile test]] is recovered.
