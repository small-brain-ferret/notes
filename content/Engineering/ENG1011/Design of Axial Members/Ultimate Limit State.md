> [!NOTE] Ultimate limit state
> Design against collapse.
> A principal limit state criteria (see [[Limit state design]])
> Also known as the **strength limit state**, it ensures extreme loads can be safely carried by a structure, without failure or collapse.
>
> Acting stresses < allowable stresss
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \text{Load} & =1.2\text{DL}+1.5\text{LL} \\ \\
>
> \sigma_{\text{tension}}\left( =\frac{P}{A} \right) & <\sigma_{\text{yield}} \\ \\
>
> F_{\text{comp}} & <P_{\text{euler}}\text{ and }\sigma_{\text{yield}}\times A \\ \\
>
> \sigma_{\text{bend}}\left( =\frac{My}{I} \right) & <\sigma_{\text{yield}}
> \end{align}
> $$
