> [!NOTE] buckling
> a state of instability where there is a sudden change in shape (deformation) of a structural member under increasing compression loading.
>
> Occurs as a result of axial loading.
>
> Once a [[strut|column]] buckles, it curves and experiences bending stresses.
>
> These stresses become greater than the axial load stresses.

Once a [[ductility|ductile]] column buckles, it may continue to carry some load. However, because buckling causes excessive deformation and instability, the critical buckling load is treated as the maximum safe load when buckling is the failure mode that happens at the lowest load.

$$
L_{\text{max axial}}=P_{cr}=P_{b}=P_{e}
$$

Where $P_{cr}=$ the critical/[[Euler-Bernoulli Beam Bending equation|Euler]] buckle load.
