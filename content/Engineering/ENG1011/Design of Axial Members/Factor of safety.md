> [!NOTE] factor of safety
> Useful for [[Limit state design]]
>
> - The higher the factor of safety, the more conservative and robust the design
> - A $[SF]$ > 1 indicates a safe system and $[SF]$ < 1 indicates failure
>
> $$
> [SF]_{\text{strength}} = \frac{[\text{load or stress carrying capacity}]}{[\text{actual load or stress}]}
> $$
>
> $$
> [SF]_{\text{deformation}} = \frac{[\text{allowable deflection or deformation}]}{[\text{actual deflection or deformation}]}
> $$

- Represents a safety margin between the actual [[load]], [[stress]] or deformation of a system, and the load, stress or deformation limit that would cause failure according to the design criteria
