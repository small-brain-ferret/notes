An important consideration for [[design of axially loaded members]].

[[limit state]] design (LSD) requires a structure to satisfy two principal limit state criteria:

| **[[Ultimate Limit State]]**                                                                                                                | **[[Serviceability Limit State]]**                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Strength (yield, [[buckling]])<br>Stability against overturning and sway<br>Fracture due to fatigue<br>Plastic collapse<br>Brittle Fracture | Deflection<br>Vibration<br>Fatigue checks (including reparable damage due to fatigue<br>Corrosion <br>Fire |

When applying limit state design, partial factors are applied to **increase** the assumed loading action and to **reduce** the assumed material strength, in order to reduce the risk of failure.

[[Factor of safety]] on loads in structural building design can be as high as 1.5, while on resistance (material strengths) as low as 0.6.
