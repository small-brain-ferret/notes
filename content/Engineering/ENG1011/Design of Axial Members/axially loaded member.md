> [!NOTE] axially loaded member
> a member, where all external forces act along its longitudinal axis.
> a [[two-force members|two-force member]] in [[equilibrium of a rigid body|equilibrium]] is always an axially loaded member.
> Generally found in the form of [[strut|struts]] and [[Tie|ties]], [[strut|columns]] in buildings, [[planar truss|truss]] members in building, etc.
