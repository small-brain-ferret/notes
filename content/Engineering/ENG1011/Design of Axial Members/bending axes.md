> [!NOTE] bending/buckling axes
>
> - when we define '[[Second moment of Area (I)- moment of inertia]]' we generally do so about two axes - the 'xx' and 'yy', which are 90 degrees apart
> - the axes lines divides the region of the member experiencing compression from the region experiences tension stresses
>
> ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-20 at 07.28.11.png|200]]
> ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-20 at 07.28.24.png|200]]
>
> - when it comes to [[buckling]], a section will buckle at its least stiff axes, everything else is the same
> - buckling occurs as a result of axial loading
> - [[bending]] is a result of transverse loading
