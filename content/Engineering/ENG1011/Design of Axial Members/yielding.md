> [!NOTE] Yielding\
> Yielding is the point at which a material begins to undergo [[plastic deformation at an atomic level|plastic deformation]].
>
> It occurs when the applied stress reaches the material’s [[yield stress]].
>
> Before yielding, the material behaves elastically and generally returns to its original shape when the load is removed.
>
> After yielding begins, removing the load will not fully restore the member to its original shape, leaving permanent deformation.
>
> In structural design, yielding is often treated as a [[design for axial tension strength|strength limit state]] because excessive permanent deformation can make a member unsafe or [[serviceability limit design|unserviceable]].
