> [!NOTE] Calculate
> $\text{First moment of Area}=\text{Area}\times \text{distance}$
> The measure of distribution of an area relative to an axis or line.

It is used to find the centroid (centre of mass) of an area.

Imagine a shape cut from stiff paper. It will balance perfectly on its centroid point.

See [[Second moment of Area (I)- moment of inertia]].
