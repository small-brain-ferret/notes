See [[First moment of Area]].

> [!NOTE] Calculate
> $\text{First moment of Area}=\text{Area}\times \text{distance}^2$
> $I=\int_{A} y^2 dA$
>
> Where
> $y$ is the distance from the centroidal or neutral axis to the area $dA$.

The measure of distribution of a member's cross-sectional area from its bending (neutral/centroid) axis.

$I$ for different cross-sections:
[[I for a Rectangular Section]]

It describes the bending stiffness of a member, and it is used when calculating [[buckling]] strength, beam/[[strut]] bending strength and beam deflection.
