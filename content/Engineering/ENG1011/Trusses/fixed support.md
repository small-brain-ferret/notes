A type of [[Supports|support]].

> [!NOTE]
> A 2D fixed support reacts against the connecting member such that it:
>
> - cannot rotate
> - cannot translate horizontally
> - cannot translate vertically
>
> Thus, it provides 3 unknown reaction forces - $M$, $F_x$, $F_y$.
