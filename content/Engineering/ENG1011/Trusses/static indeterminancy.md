> [!NOTE]
> A statically indeterminate body is one that is:
>
> 1. stable, but
> 2. all unknown reactive forces can't be determined from the [[equilibrium]] equations alone.
>
> Hence, they cannot be solved without the use of additional information/equations.

# Example

- a fixed support - $A_{x}$, $A_y$, $M_A$
- a pinned support - $B_x$, $B_y$
- Equations - $\sum M=0$, $\sum F_{x}=0$, $\sum F_{y}=0$
  5 unknowns, 3 equations.
  ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-17 at 14.39.51.png]]

# Example

- 2 pinned supports - $A_{x}$, $A_y$, $B_x$, $B_y$
- Equations - $\sum M=0$, $\sum F_{x}=0$, $\sum F_{y}=0$
  4 unknowns, 3 equations.
  ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-17 at 14.43.02.png]]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A body on supports can either be stable or unstable.
>
> Stable systems can either be [[Static determinancy|statically determinate]] or [[static indeterminancy|statically indeterminate]].
