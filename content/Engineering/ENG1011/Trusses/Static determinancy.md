> [!NOTE]
> A statically determinate body is one that is:
>
> 1. stable, and
> 2. all unknown reactive forces can be determined from the [[equilibrium of a rigid body|equations of equilibrium]] alone.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A body on supports can either be stable or unstable.
>
> Stable systems can either be [[Static determinancy|statically determinate]] or [[static indeterminancy|statically indeterminate]].
