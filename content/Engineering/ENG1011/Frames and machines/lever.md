> [!NOTE] levers
>
> - a member that pivots from a fixed point ([[fulcrum]])
> - modifies an input force to produce an output force that resists the [[load]]
> - they are used to provide a [[mechanical advantage]] like many other [[mechanisms]]
>
> $$
> F_{\text{input}}L_{\text{input arm}}=F_{\text{load}}L_{\text{load arm}}
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{MA} =\frac{F_{\text{load}}}{F_{\text{input}}}=\frac{L_{\text{input arm}}}{L_{\text{load arm}}}
> $$
>
> ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-21 at 18.08.11.png]]

The relative positioning of the [[fulcrum]], the input force, and output force, defines the class of the lever.

# Example

Pliers are used to grip onto a rod as shown.
80N are applied to each handle.
Determine the force applied on the rod by each of the plier jaws.
Additionally, calculate the forces in the pin at point A.
Finally, determine the [[mechanical advantage]] of this simple machine.
![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-21 at 18.09.48.png]]

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-21 at 18.11.22.png]]

$$
\begin{align}
\sum F_{x} & =0 \\
A_{x} & =0 \\
 \\
\sum F_{y} & =0 \\
-F+A_{y}-80 & =0 \\
-F+A_{y} & =80 \\
A_{y} & =80+F \\
 \\
\sum M_{A} & =0 \\
F \times 35-80 \times 95 & =0 \\
F & =217.1429\text{ N} \\
 \\
A_{y} & =80+217.1429 \\
 & =297.1429\text{ N} \\
  \\
 \text{MA} & =\frac{F_{\text{output}}}{F_{\text{input}}} \\
  & =\frac{217.1429}{80} \\
   & =2.7143 \\
\end{align}
$$
