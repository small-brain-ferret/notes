> [!NOTE] Structures vs machines
> Structures: usually fixed in position and designed to support applied [[load|loads]]
>
> Machines: contain moving parts and perform useful mechanical work by transforming input forces and movement into a desired set of output forces and movement
>
> ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-21 at 15.23.58.png]]

> [!NOTE] machines and mechanisms
> Machines make work easier through [[mechanisms]] that transform input forces and movement into a desired set of output forces and movement. ([[lever]], pulley, wheel/gear, inclined plane, screw, wedge).
>
