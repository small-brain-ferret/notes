Change in velocity over time, $\frac{\text{d}v}{\text{d}t}$.

Dimension:

$$
\frac{[L]}{[T]^2}
$$

a = 0 means at rest or constant velocity.
a $\neq$ 0 means changing velocity.

Links the field of [[Kinetics]] to [[Kinematics]].
