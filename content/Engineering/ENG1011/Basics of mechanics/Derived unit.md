> [!NOTE] Derived unit
> Derived units in the SI are formed by powers, products, or quotients of the [[SI base units]].
>
> eg.
>
> - Speed - $\frac{dx}{dt}$ (m/s), $\frac{[L]}{[T]}$
> - Area (m$^2$), $[L]^2$
> - Density (kg/m$^3$), $\frac{[M]}{[L]^3}$
