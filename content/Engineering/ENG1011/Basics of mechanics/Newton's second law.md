[[Newton's first law]] is just a special case of this law.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A [[particle models|particle]] acted on by a force will [[acceleration(s)|accelerate]] in the direction of the [[force]].
>
> The magnitude of the acceleration is proportional to the magnitude of the force and inversely proportional to the mass of the particle.
>
> $$
> \sum F = ma
> $$
