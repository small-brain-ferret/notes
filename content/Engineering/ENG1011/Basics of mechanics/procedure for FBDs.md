See [[Free-body diagrams]].

#### Step 1: Identify the system

This could be a single object or a combination of components.

Consider an imaginary boundary around the system.

We may not always be able to identify all [[force]]. Thus, choose which part of the system to include as your free body for best analysis.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-11 at 13.40.36.png]]

#### Step 2: Draw a diagram of the free-body system

Everything inside the boundary, including all the relevant components and their relative positions.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-11 at 13.40.54.png]]

#### Step 3: Identify all forces and loads acting _on the system_

Includes forces such as weight, friction, tension, pressure, etc.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-11 at 13.41.58.png]]

#### Step 4: Establish a coordinate system

Define the direction of forces and loads.
