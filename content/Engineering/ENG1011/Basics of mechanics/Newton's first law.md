A special case of [[Newton's second law]].

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Also known as the law of [[inertia]]. If a [[particle models|particle]] is at rest, or moving with constant velocity, it will remain at rest, or continue to move with constant velocity, unless acted on by a [[force]].
