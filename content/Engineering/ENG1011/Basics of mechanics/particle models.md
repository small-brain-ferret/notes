> [!NOTE] particle
> A particle has mass but the size can be neglected.
>
> A mechanics problem is simplified when a body is modelled as a particle, since the geometry of the body is not considered in the analysis.
>
> e.g. The size of earth is insignificant compared to the size of its orbit, therefore, earth can be modelled as a particle when studying orbital motion.
>
> e.g. Three [[force]] act on a ring. We can assume the ring to be a particle since the line of actions of the forces all meet at a point. i.e. concurrent forces.
