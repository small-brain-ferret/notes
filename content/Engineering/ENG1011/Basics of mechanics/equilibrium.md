An object is in equilibrium when the sum of all the external forces acting on the object equals zero, i.e. [[Resultant (net) force]] = 0. All the horizontal components and verticle components add to 0.

There are two possible states of equilibrium:

- [[static equilibrium]]
- [[dynamic equilibrium]]

see [[equilibrium of a rigid body]] for equations of equilibrium.

> [!NOTE]
> If a system is in equilibrium, then any subset of that system will also be in equilibrium.
