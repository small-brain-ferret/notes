A state of [[equilibrium]], where the body is at rest, that is:

$$
\begin{align}
v=0 \\
a=0
\end{align}
$$

# Example

The rope and ring system shown is in equilibrium. Assume there is no horizontal [[reaction]] at the ring. Determine the force F.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-11 at 15.28.35.png]]

#### Step 1: Use $\sum F_{x}=0$ to determine the unknown force.

$$
\begin{align}
F_{R, \ x} & =\sum F_{x}=0 \\
0 & =-6\cos(60^\circ)+F \cos(45^\circ) \\
F & =4.2426\text{ kN}
\end{align}
$$
