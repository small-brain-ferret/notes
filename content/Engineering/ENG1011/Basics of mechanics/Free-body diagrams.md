> [!NOTE]
> An essential tool in engineering, especially in mechanics.
>
> Provides a graphical representation of a system subjected to forces or loads.
>
> They are used to:
>
> - represent the free body isolated from its surroundings
> - keep track of forces and loads
> - visualize the problem
>
> If a system is in [[equilibrium]], any FBD of that system will also be in equillibrium.

There is a general [[procedure for FBDs]].
