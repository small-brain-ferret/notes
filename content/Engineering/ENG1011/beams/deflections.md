> [!NOTE] deflections
> When designing a [[beam]] andstructures in general, we need to consider [[serviceability limit design|serviceability requirements]] and its strength requirements.
>
> Deflection (y) equals:
>
> $$
> y=EIK=\int \int K=\int \int \frac{M}{EI}
> $$
>
> The main serviceability requirement is deflection.
>
> The limit state design case against excessive deflection is called [[Serviceability Limit State]].
>
> [[deflection limits]] are set to ensure the functional performance of a structure:
>
> 1. to minimise the perception of excessive movements by the occupants
> 2. to limit the unsightly appearance of sagging
> 3. to ensure the performance of attached connected services, machinery, cladding, ceilings, walls, and prevent unintended load paths
