> [!NOTE]
> Deflection limits are set according to a beam span. They cary depending on the purpose and location of a [[beam]] and its [[Supports|support]] conditions.
>
> ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-21 at 15.02.54.png]]
>
> deflections are limited to ensure the functional performance of a structure:
>
> 1. to minimise the perception of excessive movements by the occupants
> 2. to limit the unsightly appearance of sagging
> 3. to ensure the performance of attached connected services, machinery, cladding, ceilings, walls, and prevent unintended load paths
