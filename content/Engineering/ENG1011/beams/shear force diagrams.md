> [!NOTE] SFDs
> A SFD is a plot of the cumulative vertical forces, $\sum F_{y}$, as a function of the distance along the [[beam]].
>
> - horizontal except where load is applied
> - a point load correponds to a sudden drop in the [[shear]], and a [[distributed loads|uniformly distributed load]] results in a linear reduction in the shear
> - the shear force must return to zero when you reach the end of the beam because of equilibrium

> [!NOTE] General steps for finding SFDs using FBDs
>
> Step 1: Use [[equilibrium of a rigid body|equations of equilibrium]] to find the reactions
> Step 2: Create an [[Free-body diagrams|FBD]], cutting the beam at the point you want the value of V. Consider equilibrium, $\sum F_{y}=0$ and solve for V.
> The more general case is to cut the beam at an arbitrary distance $x$

> [!NOTE] General steps for finding SFDs without FBDs
>
> 1. Do not use the + or - sign of the [[shear]] force as the basis for plotting the diagram
> 2. Starting from the left, follow the magnitude of the vertical reactions and loads.
