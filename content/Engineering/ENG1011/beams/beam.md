> [!NOTE] beam
> a horizontal member supporting vertical [[load|loads]].
> The most common structural form.
>
> Depending on how they're [[Supports|supported]], and what load they carry, the pattern and value of their internal actions will be different.
>
> The [[Ultimate Limit State]] design requires us to find where the most [[stress|stressed]] part of the beam is.
>
> A beam develops [[bending]] stresses and [[shear]] stresses. Bending stresses are more critical for design.
>
> We base our beam design on these stresses.
