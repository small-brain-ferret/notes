> [!NOTE] neutral axis
> The line on the section where the [[strain]] and [[stress]] is zero.
>
> ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-21 at 00.25.33.png]]
>
> Above the NA, all the beam is in compression. Above the NA, all the beam is in tension.

> [!NOTE] Locating the NA
> To locate the NA, use the condition, that for a cross-section in equilibrium with [[bending]] moment only, T + C =0.
>
> That is, $\int y \ dA=0$.
>
> This is the [[First moment of Area]] equation where y is the distance from the neutral axis of the area element dA.
>
> $$
> y_{c}=\sum \frac{A_{i}y_{i}}{A_{\text{section}}}=\frac{{A_{1}y_{1}+A_{2}y_{2}+A_{3}y_{3}+\dots}}{A_{\text{section}}}
> $$
>
> Where
> $y_i$ is the distance of the [[centroidal axis]] of each rectangular area, $A_i$, measured from the section top or bottom
> $y_c$ is the position of the neutral axis, measured from a datum point, usually the section top or bottom.
>
> ![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-21 at 01.13.17.png]]
>
> 1. Choose a base line/datum
> 2. divide the shape into rectangles/circles
> 3. apply the formula
