Recognising [[two-force members]] and three-force members is critical in [[equilibrium of a rigid body]] because they allow us to simplify the analysis of a system.

> [!NOTE]
> A three-force member only has three forces applied. The member must satify the following necessary condition to achieve equilibrium:
>
> - moment equilibrium - the three forces must form a cocurrent or parallel force system

The system must also satisfy $\sum F_{R}=0$ and $\sum M_{R}=0$ for equilibrium.
