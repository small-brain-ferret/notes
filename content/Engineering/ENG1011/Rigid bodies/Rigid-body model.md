> [!NOTE]
> A rigid body can be considered as a combination of many [[particle models|particles]].
>
> The shape of the body does not change before and after applying the loads.
>
> A non-deformable body does not require knowledge of the type of material it was made from.
>
> In most cases, the actual deformation occuring in structures, machines, and other bodies are relatively small, which makes the rigid-body assumption suitable for analysis.
