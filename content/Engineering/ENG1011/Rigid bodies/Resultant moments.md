A body may be subjected to multiple [[forces]] ($F_1$, $F_2$, ...).

These forces may induce [[moments]].

The sum of these moments about point O ($z$-axis) is the resultant moment $(M_R)_{O}$.

$$
(M_R)_{O}=\sum Fd
$$

# Example Q

Determine the resultant moment about point $O$.
![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-16 at 20.35.59.png]]

##### Step 1: Identify all forces that act not on the line of action of the force

- 50N downwards, cw moment, 2m vertically to the line of action of the force
- 20N to the right, acw moment, 3 sin(30) vertically to the line of action of the force
- 40N downwards, cw moment, 4+3cos(30) horizontally to the line of action of the force

##### Step 2: Find the sum of all moments

$$
\begin{align}
(M_R)_{O} & =\sum Fd \\
 & =-(50)(2)+(20)(3\sin(30^{\circ}))-(40)(4+3\cos(30^{\circ})) \\
  & =-333.9230\text{ Nm}
\end{align}
$$

$\therefore 333.9230$ Nm about $O$
