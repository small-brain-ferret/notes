> [!NOTE]
> A body may be subjected to a loading that is distributed over its surface.
>
> This loading can be represented as an equivalent [[concentrated forces|point force]], which is the total force represented by the distributed force and is located at the centroid to create the same [[moment]] as the distributed force.

> [!NOTE] non-uniformly distributed load
> The distributed load doesn't necessarily need to be uniform.
>
> It can be represented by any function.
>
> Integration is typically required to determine the equivalent point force and the centroid location.
