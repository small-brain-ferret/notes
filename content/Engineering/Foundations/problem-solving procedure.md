> [!NOTE] Problem
> Being in a state different to that desired.

> [!NOTE] Solution
> That which transfers the existing state to some other state. Ideally, a desired state.

Complex issues may present a vast number of solutions that necessitate an endless number of considerations.

Thus, making thoughtful and deliberate problem-solving decisions is crucial to determining a feasible solution.
