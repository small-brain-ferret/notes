A [[Resultant (net) force]] can be determined via this method.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-11 at 12.08.37.png]]

Two useful trigonometric laws used in combination with this method are the [[Law of cosines]] and [[Law of sines]].

# Example

Determine the resultant vector and the angle $\alpha$ from the horizontal.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-11 at 12.15.45.png]]

#### Step 1: Perform graphic vector addition using the parallelogram/triangle method

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-06-11 at 12.16.34.png]]

#### Step 2: Apply the [[Law of cosines]] to determine the unknown force

Let A = 2 kN, B = 6 kN, C = $F_{R}$.

$$
\begin{align}
C & =\sqrt{ A^2 + B^2 - 2AB \cos(c)} \\
F_{R} & =\sqrt{ 2^2 +6^2 -2(2)(6)\cos(105^{\circ})} \\
 & =6.7979\ \text{kN}
\end{align}
$$

#### Step 3: Apply the [[Law of sines]] to determine the unknown angle

$$
\begin{align}
\frac{C}{\sin(c)} & =\frac{B}{\sin(b)} \\
\frac{6.7979}{\sin(105^\circ)} & =\frac{6}{\sin(\theta)} \\
\theta & =58.49^\circ \\
\alpha & =180^\circ-45^\circ-\theta \\
 & =76.51^\circ
\end{align}
$$
