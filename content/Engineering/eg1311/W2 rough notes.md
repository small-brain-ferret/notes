Newton's 2nd Law

$$
\mathbf{F}=\frac{d(m\mathbf{v})}{dt}=m\mathbf{a}
$$

Work energy

$$
W=\int _{d\mathbf{s}\in C}F\cdot d\mathbf{s}=\mathbf{F}\cdot s
$$
