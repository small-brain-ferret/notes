> [!NOTE] ideal ammeters
> An ideal ammeter measures current without creating a voltage drop.
> It has zero resistance:
>
> $$
> R_{AM} \to 0
> $$
>
> Using Ohm's law:
>
> $$
> V = IR
> $$
>
> so:
>
> $$
> R \to 0
> \Rightarrow
> V \to 0
> $$
>
> Therefore, an ideal ammeter behaves like a **short circuit**.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Ideal ammeter} \rightarrow \text{short circuit}}
> $$
