> [!NOTE] ideal voltmeters
> An ideal voltmeter measures voltage without affecting the circuit.
> It has extremely large resistance:
>
> $$
> R_{VM} \to \infty
> $$
>
> Using Ohm's law:
>
> $$
> I = \frac{V}{R}
> $$
>
> so:
>
> $$
> R \to \infty
> \Rightarrow
> I \to 0
> $$
>
> Therefore, an ideal voltmeter behaves approximately like an **open circuit**.
>
> $$
> \boxed{\text{Ideal voltmeter} \rightarrow \text{open circuit}}
> $$
