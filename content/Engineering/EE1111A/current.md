> [!NOTE]
> A measure of flow of [[electric charge]].
>
> Charge carriers can be [[electron|electrons]] in a [[conductors|conductor]] or ions in an electrolyte.
>
> Conventional direction of positive current is the flow of positive charge.
>
> "How many coulombs per second of charge is flowing past given point in the wire/how many electrons per second are flowing past?"
>
> The unit is amperes, equivalent to:
>
> $$
> \frac{\text{Coulomb}}{\text{sec}}=\frac{\text{C}}{\text{s}}
> $$
