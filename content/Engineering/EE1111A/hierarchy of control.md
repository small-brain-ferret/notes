> [!NOTE]
> A system widely used in industries, safety organisations to minimize or elimiate exposure to hazards.
>
> Following the hierarchy from top to bottom when considering hazards leads to the implementation of safer systems with reduced risk of illness or injury.

# Elimination

- The physical removal of the hazard. The most effective.

# Substitution

- replace something that produces a hazard with something that does not produce a hazard

# Engineering controls

- implemented to prevent a hazard from coming into contact with the worker/people
- eg. fume cupboards, smoke absorbers, electrical conduits, safety guards

# Administrative controls

- prevent exposure to hazards by attempting to change the way people work
- eg. changes in procedure, instituting standard operating procedures, employee training, installation of signs and warnings

# PPE

- least preferred form of control
- equipment that can be worn by the worker to provide protection
