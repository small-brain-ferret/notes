> [!NOTE]
> A measure of energy transferred per unit [[electric charge|charge]] when the charge is moved from one point to another.
>
> The unit of voltage is volt, equivalent to:
>
> $$
> \frac{\text{Joules}}{\text{Coulomb}}\to \frac{\text{J}}{\text{C}}
> $$

Voltage is always measured between two points of labelled polarities, + and -.

A [[battery]] with higher voltage will drive higher amount of electricity through the same external conductor.

chemical reaction inside a battery creates a potential difference (voltage) between battery terminals.

- When a [[conductors|conducting]] material is connected externally between the terminals of a battery, current flows through the circuit
