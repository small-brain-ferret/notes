> [!NOTE] Node Voltage
> The [[voltage]] at a node with respect to a reference node.
> e.g. the conductor line at the bottom is taken as a reference, identified by attaching the 'circuit ground' (triangle) symbol to that node.
>
> $$
> V_{\text{branch}}=V_{x}-V_{y}
> $$
>
> Where $V_x$ is the node voltage at the tail of [[current]] arrow, $V_y$ is at the tip of the current arrow.
>
> ![[Attachments/Pasted image 20260911134841.png]]
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> V_{R_{1}} & =V_{a}-V_{b} \\
> V_{R_{2}} & =V_{b}-V_{c} \\ 
> V_{R_{3}} & =V_{b}-0 \\
> \end{align}
> $$
>
> $V_{R_{1}} =V_{a}-V_{b}$ implies:
>
> - left of $R_{1}$ is +ve, right of $R_{1}$ is -ve
> - current points from $V_{a}$ to $V_b$
