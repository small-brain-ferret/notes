> [!NOTE] Definition
> One of [[Simpson's Rule(s)]].
>
> Derived from fitting a cubic function to four points on a function, $f(x)$.

# Single application

Consider:
Four equally spaced points ($x_1$, $f(x_1)$), ($x_2$, $f(x_2)$), ($x_3$, $f(x_3)$), and ($x_4$, $f(x_4)$) separated by the interval $h=x_{2}-x_1=x_{3}-x_{2}=x_{4}-x_{3}$, are fitted with a cubic function. The area under the cubic is the estimate, $I$, of the integral.

$$
I=\frac{3h}{8}(f(x_{1})+3f(x_{2})+3f(x_{3})+f(x_{4}))
$$
