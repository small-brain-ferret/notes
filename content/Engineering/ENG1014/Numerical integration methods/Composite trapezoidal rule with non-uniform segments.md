> [!NOTE] Definition
> The [[Composite trapezoidal rule]] for non-uniform segments of different widths.
>
> Given by:
>
> $$
> I=\sum_{i=2}^n \left( \frac{x_{i}-x_{i-1}}{2} \right) (f(x_{{i-1}})+f(x_{i}))
> $$
>
> Where $n$ is the number of points, $x_{i-1}$ and $x_i$ are a pair of adjacent points.

This is used for when we are not able to have equally spaced data.
