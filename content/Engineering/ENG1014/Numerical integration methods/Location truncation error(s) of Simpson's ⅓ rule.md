> [!NOTE]
> As number of segments increase (width of segments decrease), the difference between the composite parabolas and the actual function gets smaller.
>
> $$
> E_{\text{T, local}}=-\frac{1}{90}f^(4)(\xi)\ h^5
> $$
>
> In other words
>
> $$
> E_{\text{T, local}}=O(h^5)
> $$
>
> And,
>
> $$
> E_{\text{T, global}}=O(h^4)
> $$

Note: if a function has a fourth derivative that is 0, [[Simpson's ⅓ Rule]] will have no error. Thus, cubic functions and polynomials of lower order will be integrated exactly.
