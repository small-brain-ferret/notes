> [!NOTE] Definition
> The sum over all of the [[Location truncation error of trapezoidal rule]].
>
> These can be both positive and negative, so errors are often cancellative instead of cumulative.
>
> $$
> E_{\text{T, global}}=O(h^2)
> $$

i.e.
If the step size if halved in the [[Trapezoidal Rules]], then $E_{\text{T, global}}$ will be $\frac{1}{4}$ its original value.

It step size is tripled, $E_{\text{T, global}}$ will be 9-fold larger.
