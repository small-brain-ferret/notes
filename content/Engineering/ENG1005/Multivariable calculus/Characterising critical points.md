After finding the location of [[Critical points]], we would like to characterise them.
This may be used in [[Question Type - Finding the absolute min or max of a multivariable function]]).

For functions of a single variable $f(x)$

| Slope     | before | At  | After | 2nd diff |
| --------- | ------ | --- | ----- | -------- |
| Local max | +ve    | 0   | -ve   | <0       |
| Local min | -ve    | 0   | +ve   | >0       |

For multivariable functions, a critical point can be characterised as a [[Local maximum (MVF)]], [[Local minimum (MVF)]], or a [[saddle (MVF)]].

## Key Idea

At a critical point:

$$
\nabla f = 0
$$

- all directional second derivatives negative $\to$ local maximum
- all directional second derivatives positive $\to$ local minimum
- mixed signs $\to$ saddle point

The [[Hessian Matrix]] can also be used in characterising critical points.
