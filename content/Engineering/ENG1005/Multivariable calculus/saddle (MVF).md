Important to understand [[Characterising critical points]].

A saddle point occurs when a [[critical point]] behaves like a minimum in some directions and a maximum in others.

At a saddle point:

$$
\nabla f = 0
$$

but the second directional derivatives have mixed signs.

That is, there exists at least one direction $\hat{u}$ such that:

$$
\frac{d^2F}{du^2} > 0
$$

and at least one other direction such that:

$$
\frac{d^2F}{du^2} < 0
$$

So the surface curves upward in some directions and downward in others.

This means the point is neither a local maximum nor a local minimum.
