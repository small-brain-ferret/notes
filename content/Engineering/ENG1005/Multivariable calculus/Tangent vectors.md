> [!NOTE] Definition
> A tangent vector is a vector tangential to a curve at a given point.

![[Attachments/Screenshot 2026-05-06 at 06.41.52.png]]

$$
\begin{align}
\text{tangent vector} & =\lim_{ \delta t \to 0 } \frac{\delta x}{\delta t} \\
 & =\lim_{ \delta t\to 0 } \frac{x(t+\delta t)-x(t)}{\delta t} \\
  & =\frac{dx}{dt}
\end{align}
$$
