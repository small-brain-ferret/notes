You can parameterise curves such as [[circle]], [[ellipse]] and [[hyperbola]].

One alternative is to write $y=f(x)$. This is an [[explicit representation]] of the curve.

$$
\begin{align}
\text{implicit}: & \ x^2+y^2=25 \\
\text{explicit}: & \ y=\sqrt{ 25-x^2 }\ \ \text{{and}}\ \ y=-\sqrt{ 25-x^2 }
\end{align}
$$

Another alternative is a [[parametric representation]].
Think of a curve as tracing out the path of a point, ie:

$$
\begin{align}
x(t) & =f(t) \\
y(t) & =g(t) \\ 
z(t) & =h(t)
\end{align}
$$

Parameterisations are not unique.
