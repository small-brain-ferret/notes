For functions of a single variable f(x), local maxima/minima have zero slope:
$\frac{df}{dx} = 0$
In general, point with $\frac{df}{dx} = 0$ is known as a **stationary point**.

For functions of several variables $f(x,y)$, local maxima/minima have zero slope in every direction:
$\nabla f=0$
In general, point with $\nabla f=0$ is (see [[The gradient - nabla f]]) known as a **critical point.**

It is important to understand the process of [[Characterising critical points]].
