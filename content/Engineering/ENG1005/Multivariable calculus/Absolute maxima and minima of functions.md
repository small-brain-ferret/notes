The absolute maximum/minimum is the maximum/minimum of a function on a domain, distinct from Local maximum/minimum (see [[Characterising critical points]]).

[[Lagrange multipliers]] are usually useful in finding maxima and minima subject to constraints.

[[Question Type - Finding the absolute min or max of a multivariable function]]

Principles:

1. IF we do not have a local maximum/minimum, then the absolute maximum/minimum must be on the boundaries.
2. Saddle points cannot be absolute maxima or minima.
