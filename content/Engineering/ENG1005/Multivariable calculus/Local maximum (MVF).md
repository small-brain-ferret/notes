A type of [[Critical points]]. Important to understand [[Characterising critical points]].

For a function $f(x,y)$, define $F(u)$ as the value of $f(x,y)$ seen along some direction $u$.

At a local maximum, the slope changes from positive to zero to negative:

$$
\frac{dF}{du} > 0 \;\to\; \frac{dF}{du} = 0 \;\to\; \frac{dF}{du} < 0
$$

Therefore the derivative is decreasing:

$$
\frac{d}{du}\left(\frac{dF}{du}\right) = \frac{d^2F}{du^2} < 0
$$

This must hold for all directions $u$. In particular:

$$
\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2} < 0, \qquad \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2} < 0
$$

but the condition is stronger than either one alone.
