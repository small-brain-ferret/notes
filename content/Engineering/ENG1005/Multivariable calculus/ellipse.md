Standard form:

$$
\frac{(x-x_0)^2}{a^2}+\frac{(y-y_0)^2}{b^2}=1  
$$

Properties:

- centre: $(x_0,y_0)$
- semi-axis lengths:
- horizontal: $a$
- vertical: $b$

Example:

$$
\frac{(x-1)^2}{4}+\frac{(y+1)^2}{9}=1  
$$

Properties:

- centre: $(1,-1)$
- horizontal semi-axis: $2$
- vertical semi-axis: $3$
