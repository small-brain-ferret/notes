> [!NOTE] Integral of cot(x)
>
> $$
> \int \cot(x)\,dx=\ln(|\sin(x)|)+C
> $$

Derivation:

$$
\begin{align}
\int \cot(x)\,dx & =\frac{\cos(x)}{\sin(x)}\,dx \\
\text{Let }u=\sin(x)\implies du=\cos(x) \\
\int \cot(x)\,dx & =\int \frac{1}{u}\,du \\
 & =\ln(|u|)+C \\
  & =\ln(|\sin (x)|)+C
\end{align}
$$
