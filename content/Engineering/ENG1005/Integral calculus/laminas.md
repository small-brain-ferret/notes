> [!NOTE] laminas
> Laminas are theoretical objects with zero thickness but positive mass.
>
> For a lamina R of density $\rho(x,y)$, we can compute its:
>
> $$
> \text{mass, }m=\int \int_{R} \rho(x,y)  \, dA
> $$
>
> physical centroid, $\bar{x},\bar{y}$, where
>
> $$
> \bar{x}=\frac{1}{m} \int \int_{R} x \cdot \rho(x,y) \, dA 
> $$
>
> $$
> \bar{y}=\frac{1}{m} \int \int_{R} y \cdot \rho(x,y) \, dA 
> $$
