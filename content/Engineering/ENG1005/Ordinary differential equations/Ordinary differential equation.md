> [!NOTE] Definition
> An ordinary differential equation (ODE) is an equation involving a dependent variable $y$ that is a function of one independent variable $x$.
> ie. $y(x)$ and one or more of its derivatives. eg. $\frac{dy}{dx},\ \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}$
> Example of an ODE:
>
> $$
> \frac{d^2y}{dx^2} + x \frac{dy}{dx} = -y e^{x+y}
> $$
>
> "Ordinary" means it depends on one independent variable.
