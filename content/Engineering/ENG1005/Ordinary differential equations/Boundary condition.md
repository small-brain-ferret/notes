> [!NOTE] Definition
> Boundary conditions are imposed at two different values of the independent variable.
>
> ie. for an ODE with variables $x$, $y$:
> $y=0$ at $x=0$ and $y=3$ at $x=0.5$ forms a set of boundary conditions.

A [[boundary value problem (BVP)]] is an [[Ordinary differential equation]] with boundary conditions. These will result in a particular solution rather than a general one.
