> [!NOTE] Order of equation
> The highest derivative that appears in the [[Ordinary differential equation|ODE]].
> Number of arbitrary constants = order = number of [[Initial condition|initial conditions]]/[[Boundary condition|boundary conditions]] needed.
>
> eg. $\frac{d^2z}{dt^2}=-g$ is second order.
> eg. $\frac{dy}{dx}+\frac{x}{y}=0$ is first order.

> [!NOTE] Linear
> Whether an equation is linear/non-linear.
> An equation is linear if it can be written in the following form:
>
> $$
> c_{n}(x) \frac{d^ny}{dx^n}+c_{n-1}(x) \frac{d^{n-1}y}{dx^{n-1}}+\dots+c_{2}(x) \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}+c_{1}(x) \frac{dy}{dx}+c_{0}(x)y=r(x)
> $$
>
> where $r(x)$ is some RHS.
> A linear ODE is homogeneous if $r(x)=0$, constant coefficient $c_i(x)=c_x$.
>
> eg. $\frac{d^2z}{dt^2}=-g$ is linear.
> eg. $\frac{dy}{dx}+\frac{x}{y}=0$ is non-linear.
