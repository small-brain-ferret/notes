[[Diagonalisation(s)]] is a useful way of rewriting a matrix in terms of its [[Eigenvalue(s)]] and [[Eigenvector(s)]].
Eigenvectors can be used for expressing matrices in a diagonlised form through [[Diagonalisation(s)]].

> [!NOTE] Definition
> An eigenvector is a vector whose direction doesn't change on transformation (although sense might change).
>
> Formally:
> A vector $\mathbf{v}\neq \mathbf{0}$ is an eigenvector of a matrix $A$ if:
>
> $$
> A\mathbf{v}=\lambda \mathbf{v}  
> $$
>
> where:
>
> - $\lambda$ is the **[[Eigenvalue(s)]]** corresponding to $\mathbf{v}$
>
> Interpretation:
>
> - Applying $A$ to $\mathbf{v}$ only **scales** it (no change in direction)
> - $\lambda$ tells you the **scaling factor**
