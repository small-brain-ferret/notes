These can be used when [[Using hyperbolic functions for integration]].
See [[Hyperbolic functions]] for more info.

> [!NOTE] Derivative of $\cosh(x)$
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \cosh(x) &= \frac12(e^x+e^{-x}) \\
> \frac{d}{dx}(\cosh x)
> &= \frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac12(e^x+e^{-x})\right] \\
> &= \frac12e^x-\frac12e^{-x} \\
> &= \sinh(x)
> \end{align}
> $$

> [!NOTE] Derivative of $\sinh(x)$
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \frac{d}{dx}(\sinh x)=\cosh(x)
> \end{align}
> $$

> [!NOTE] Derivative of $\tanh(x)$
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \frac{d}{dx}(\tanh x)=\operatorname{sech}^2(x)
> \end{align}
> $$

> [!NOTE] Derivative of $\operatorname{sech}(x)$
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \frac{d}{dx}(\operatorname{sech}x)
> =-\operatorname{sech}(x)\tanh(x)
> \end{align}
> $$

> [!NOTE] Derivative of $\operatorname{cosech}(x)$
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \frac{d}{dx}(\operatorname{cosech}x)
> =-\operatorname{cosech}(x)\coth(x)
> \end{align}
> $$

> [!NOTE] Derivative of $\coth(x)$
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \frac{d}{dx}(\coth x)
> =-\operatorname{cosech}^2(x)
> \end{align}
> $$
