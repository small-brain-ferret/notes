> The identification of fundamental elements that may be used (gears, servos, airfoils, etc)

5 axes of categorization for UAVs:

1. **Size**
   The maximum take-off weight (MTOW) is used to distinguish between micro (2-20kg), medium (20-150kg), and large(>150kg) UAVs.

2. **Mission**
   There are six basic mission types: surveillance, trasnport, combat, communications relay, support, target

3. **Capability**
   The performance of the UAV in terms of endurance, range, speed, payload mass, and operational ceiling

4. **Degree of autonomy**
   Refer to the chart developed at the US Wright-Patterson Air Force Base

5. **Aero-structural configuration**
   This ranges from conventional fuselage/wings with traditional control surfaces and blended wing-bodies, to aircrafts using things like the Coanda effect controls
