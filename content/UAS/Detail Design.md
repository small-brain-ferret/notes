> [!NOTE] Definition
> The final stage in [[General Aircraft Stages of Design]] and [[UAV Applied Stages of Design]].
>
> This is where the actual pieces to be fabricated are designed.
>
> ie. During **detail** design, the wing box will be broken down into individual ribs, spars, and skins.

Design the actual pieces to be built
Design the tooling and fabrication process
Test major items (structure, landing gear, etc)
Finalize weight and performance estimates
