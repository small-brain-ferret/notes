Before considering each component part of a UAV in [[Detail Design]], it is useful to consider key design drivers that control small/medium fixed-wing [[UAV]]s.

> [!NOTE] Note
> The key inputs to any vehicle design, whether airborne, seaborne, or landborne, are:
>
> 1. payload mass
> 2. range/endurance/cruise speed

# Landing speed as a design driver of UAV design

This controls the size and complexity of the wings, since low landing speed = large wings and/or sophisticated high-lift devices.

Small UAVs: focus on large wings and simple flaps
medium UAVs: Consider more complex high-lift devices

Considering a low MTOW, and imperfect landing fields, low landing speeds strongly impact the likelihood of damage on the airframe.

# Propulsion as a design driver of UAV design

Engines or motors have to be purchased off the shelf.

Need to seek balance between size and power. You may have something too small and underpowering, or too large and too heavy.
