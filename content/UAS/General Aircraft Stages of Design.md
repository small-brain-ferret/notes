# [[Concept design]]

For an aircraft:

- payload
- range
- speed
- cost

For an engine:

- thrust
- weight
- fuel efficiency
- cost

This is where the basic ideas of:

- configuration arrangement
- size
- weight
- performance
  are covered.

# [[Preliminary design]]

This begins when the major changes are over.

The configuration arrangement can be expected to remain about as shown on existing drawings - minor changes can occur.

Testing initiates in areas such as aerodynamics, propulsion, structures, stability and control.

**[[Lofting]]** is a key activity that occurs in this stage.

The ultimate objective during this stage is to ready the producer for full-scale development ([[Detail Design]] stage).

# [[Detail Design]]

The actual pieces to be fabricated are designed.

Example: During [[Concept design]] and [[Preliminary design]], the wing box will be designed and analysed as a whole.

During [[Detail Design]], that whole will be broken down into individual ribs, spars, and skins.

Each must be separately designed and analysed.

**[[Production design]]** is another important part of detail design.

In this stage, design may need to be modified for ease of manufacture, but design must still meet original requirements.
