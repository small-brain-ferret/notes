The fundamental hallmark of design is not analysis but synthesis. The choices of:

- mechanism types
- power sources
- setting of dimensions
- choice of features and subcomponents
- selection of materials
- manufacturing processes
  These are all acts of synthesis.
  The skilful making of decisions in these areas is the hallmark of a good designer.

This process may be thought of as being built from four fundamental components:

1. _**[[Taxonomy of UAVs]].**_
2. _**[[Morphology of UAVs]].**_
3. _**Creativity or synthesis.**_ The creation of new taxonomies, morphologies, or fundamental elements (such as the linear induction motor)
4. _**Decision making**_. The selection of the best taxonomy, morphology, and design configurations.
