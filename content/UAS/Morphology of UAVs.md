> [!NOTE] Definition
> The identification of the steps and their order of the design process.

The design team need to consider the following components:

1. _**[[Design considerations of Lifting surfaces]]**_

2. _**Control surfaces or their equivalents**_
   Typically elevators, rudders, ailerons, flaps, air-brakes.

3. _**Fuselages**_
   To house systems.

4. _**Internal structure**_
   To support all the loads seen by the vehicle.
   To connect components together.

5. _**Propulsion system**_
   Propellor, turbo-fan, or jet-based.

6. _**Energy source**_
   Generally kerosene, gas, methanol, or LiPo batteries

7. _**Command, comms**_
   Control systems, and associated on-board power system (generally supported by battery)

8. _**Payload**_
   Commonly sensors or munitions. Sometimes meds, emergency aid, or other lightweight goods.

9. _**Take-off and landing gear**_
   Generally wheels with suspension and steering, sometimes retractable.

There are a number of ways these elements can be laid out to form an aircraft. The following are consideration, in this order:

1. Number and type of engines (sinple, twin, ally three-engine, propeller)
2. Tractor or pusher propellers? Or a combination?
   This leads to an idea of the fuselage (single central, central with twin-mounted engine nacelles, twin fuselages)
3. The type of wing (low, middle high)
4. Undercarriage layout (nose-wheel, tail-wheel)
