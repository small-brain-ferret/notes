> [!NOTE] Economic view of taditional concept design
> Traditional concept design forms a business case, rather than an engineering process.
>
> The design considered after the decision to proceed can be very different from the concept design.
>
> Thus, concept designers should allow for this dislocation, so performance specifications can be delivered in practice.

In the modern [[UAV Applied Stages of Design]], the merging of the [[Concept design]] and [[Preliminary design]] eliminates [[difficulties due to conflict between economic and engineering perspectives]] to a large extent. However - it requires a greater effort to set up and develop.
