> [!NOTE] Aim
> To use efficient aerodynamic sections, together with planforms (outline of an aircraft viewed directly above/below) that balance the goal to have high aspect ratios and the wish for small bending moments.

The following approaches can be considered in deciding on a planform:

1. stresses can be carried by a series of dedicated structural elements (spars, ribs, stringers, etc)
2. the skin of the wing can be stressed, and a monocoque approach can be adopted

An important consideration for the [[Morphology of UAVs]].
